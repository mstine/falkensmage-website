<objective>
Render `mention-of` webmentions under FA essays as a "Mentioned by" row, and keep the site's own
cross-links out of the Responses section.

Purpose: 261030-kwk counted `mention-of` but did not show it, and flagged that as a decision for
Matt. A blog post citing an essay arrives as `mention-of`, so this is the kind that most often
carries a real citation. The same decision exposes a problem: `/fa/both-hands/` linking to
`/fa/labeled-as-potatoes/` already exists as a real `mention-of`, and the site's own links must
never show up as responses from other people.
Output: one new KINDS entry, an own-site filter with its own log bucket, one template loop, one
probe tuple entry, tests, and an updated coverage comment.
</objective>
