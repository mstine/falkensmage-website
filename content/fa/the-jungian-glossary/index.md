**Active Imagination** · *(Found. [IV](/fa/tarot-as-pattern-tech/), [VI](/fa/dreamwork/))* — Engaging a symbol, figure, or dream-image while awake and letting it move and speak on its own — a tarot figure, a dream pursuer, an inner advisor. Not daydreaming (you steer that); active imagination *surprises* you, and the surprise is the tell that it's real. Jung's core method for talking to the unconscious.

**Albedo** · *(Found. [III](/fa/alchemy/))* — The "whitening," the second alchemical stage. After the rot of the nigredo: the washing, the first clarity, sobriety returning. Its danger is mistaking *rinsed* for *finished.*

**Anima / Animus** · *(Found. [II](/fa/archetype/))* — The contrasexual archetype — the inner feminine (anima) in a man's psyche, inner masculine (animus) in a woman's, however you hold those. *Autonomous* — not a repressed bit of ego but a force with its own life that you build a relationship with. Bridges the personal self to the deeper Self.

**Archetype** · *(Found. [II](/fa/archetype/))* — A structure the psyche comes pre-installed with — an inherited pattern, not a learned belief. The Mother, the Trickster, the Shadow, the Descent. Jung's "two-million-year-old human." The *nouns* of the inner world.

**Citrinitas** · *(Found. [III](/fa/alchemy/))* — The "yellowing," the third alchemical stage — the one the three-color telling drops. After the washing (albedo) comes the *lighting:* the clean material glows with its own solar light, clarity warming into wisdom, the seeing finally catching fire. Being *lit,* not just *rinsed.* Still interior — the embodied return is the rubedo. (Threshold marker: the *cauda pavonis,* the peacock's tail.)

**Collective Unconscious** · *(Found. [II](/fa/archetype/))* — The shared deep layer beneath your personal unconscious, where the archetypes live. Not yours — *everyone's.* The reservoir the tarot, the chart, and dreams all draw from.

**Compensation** · *(Found. [VI](/fa/dreamwork/))* — One main job of a dream: to show you what your waking attitude is leaving out — the part of the pattern your conscious bias filters off. Dreams lean against your blind spot on purpose.

**Complex** · *(Found. [II](/fa/archetype/))* — A cluster of charged emotional material organized around an archetypal core (the mother complex, the money complex). When one "kicks in," you become someone else for a minute — reacting from the cluster, not from choice. The goal isn't to kill it; it's to make it conscious.

**Constellated** · *(Found. [IV](/fa/tarot-as-pattern-tech/))* — When an archetype gets *activated* — switched on and moving in you. A tarot card doesn't predict an event; it *constellates* an archetype, which then shows up everywhere because you finally have eyes for it.

**Ego** · *(Found. [II](/fa/archetype/))* — The internal narrator, the "I" voice running the show in your head. It wears the persona and mistakes itself for the whole self. Not the enemy — it runs the survival software — just not all of you.

**Individuation** · *(Found. [II](/fa/archetype/), [III](/fa/alchemy/))* — The lifelong process of becoming whole: taking the parts you split off (shadow, anima, the disowned) and integrating them — not by picking a side but by getting big enough to hold both. The spiral toward the Self. It never "finishes"; you spiral.

**Maya** · *(Found. [I](/fa/the-morrigan-and-grep/); Vedanta / CreativeMind)* — In the Vedantic ground beneath the Jungian work: the veil — reality's power to *conceal* (you can't see the oneness) and to *project* (you mistake your mind's construction for the outside world). Shadow work reverses both. The ancient name for what neuroscience now calls the predictive brain.

**Nigredo** · *(Found. [III](/fa/alchemy/))* — The "blackening," the first alchemical stage. The rot, the putrefaction, the dark night, the descent. Everyone wants to skip it; nobody gets to. *Solve* happens here. You cannot reach the gold without it.

**Numinous** · *(Found. [VI](/fa/dreamwork/))* — The quality of an experience that feels charged with the sacred or the uncanny — awe, dread, a weight that isn't "yours." When a dream or a symbol goes numinous, the deep layer has opened.

**Opus (Magnum Opus)** · *(Found. [III](/fa/alchemy/))* — "The Great Work." The whole alchemical process of transformation, start to gold — which Jung read as the projected map of individuation. Your life *is* the opus.

**Persona** · *(Found. [II](/fa/archetype/))* — The mask — the self you built (mostly ages 0–5) to survive your particular tribe. It worked so well you forgot it was a costume. The trouble starts when you think it's your skin.

**Personal Unconscious** · *(Found. [II](/fa/archetype/))* — Your own basement: everything you repressed building the persona, stored as emotional imprints, not ideas. Where your shadow lives. Sits on top of the collective unconscious.

**Prima Materia** · *(Found. [III](/fa/alchemy/))* — The "first matter" — the raw, base, unpromising stuff the alchemical work *starts* from. In a life: the mess, the wound, the stuck place. Lead is not a problem to discard; it's the material.

**Projection** · *(Found. [II](/fa/archetype/))* — The ego's main defense: seeing your own disowned material *out there,* in other people. The disproportionate, righteous heat you feel toward someone is almost always your shadow in their face. Every trigger is a mirror. (The flip: over-admiration is your *bright* shadow projected.)

**Rubedo** · *(Found. [III](/fa/alchemy/))* — The "reddening," the fourth and final alchemical stage. The gold, the return — the purified self brought *back into life,* embodied and full-blooded. Integration not glimpsed but lived. Red is blood, is life, is the work made flesh.

**The Self** · *(Found. [II](/fa/archetype/))* — Capital-S: the *total* you — the whole the psyche splintered into all those opposites. The center the whole spiral circles. Not the ego; the thing the ego is a small island inside of.

**Shadow** · *(Found. [II](/fa/archetype/))* — Everything you pushed underground to build the persona. **Not** mostly your darkness — mostly your *repressed brilliance:* the **bright shadow,** the too-much you buried (too loud, too fierce, too sexual, too strange). Not your wound — your *unlived life.* Where "feral" lives.

**Solve et Coagula** · *(Found. [III](/fa/alchemy/))* — "Dissolve and reconstitute." The master alchemical verb: let what's congealed come apart, so something truer can re-form. The order is non-negotiable — *solve before coagula.* No becoming the next you without letting the current one dissolve.

**Synchronicity** · *(Found. [IV](/fa/tarot-as-pattern-tech/))* — Jung's term for a meaningful coincidence — an outer event and an inner state connected by *meaning* rather than cause. His proposed mechanism for why divination works: matter and psyche meeting through the *unus mundus.* Held as a real, open mystery — not sold as proven.

**Transcendent Function** · *(Found. [VI](/fa/dreamwork/))* — What activates when conscious and unconscious start cross-pollinating — awareness entering dreams, dream-material entering waking life. Jung saw it as the psyche preparing to birth a new, more integrated self.

**Unus Mundus** · *(Found. [IV](/fa/tarot-as-pattern-tech/))* — "One world." The Hermetic/Jungian idea that psyche and matter are ultimately one fabric — the floor under synchronicity, and the cousin of *as above, so below* from [Foundation I](/fa/the-morrigan-and-grep/).
