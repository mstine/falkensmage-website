## The structural four (the architecture of every psyche)

*These aren't optional characters — they're the load-bearing structure from [Foundation II](/fa/archetype/).*

- **The Persona** — the mask built for the tribe; your social face. *Shadow:* when you mistake it for your whole self, it becomes a cage you can't see the bars of.
- **The Shadow** — everything exiled to build the persona, dark *and* bright (the buried brilliance). *Shadow of the Shadow:* projected outward onto everyone who triggers you, run as fate instead of owned as material.
- **The Anima / Animus** — the autonomous contrasexual soul-figure, the bridge to the deep. *Shadow:* possession (the hyper-critical inner judge; the emotional flood) or projection onto a partner who can't carry it.
- **The Self** — the total, whole you; the center the whole journey circles. *Shadow:* inflation — the ego mistaking *itself* for the Self (the guru disease).

## The universal cast (the collective unconscious)

- **The Great Mother** — nourishment, the womb, unconditional holding. *Shadow:* the Devouring/Terrible Mother — smothering, engulfing, refusing to let you leave.
- **The Wise Old One (Senex / Crone)** — wisdom, mentorship, the elder who's walked it. *Shadow:* rigidity, the dead hand of "we've always done it this way," wisdom turned to control.
- **The Divine Child** — new life, potential, the future self being born. *Shadow:* the eternal adolescent (*puer/puella*) who won't land, won't commit, won't grow up.
- **The Trickster** — the boundary-crosser, the rule-breaker who moves things the serious can't. *Shadow:* chaos for its own sake, betrayal, the con.
- **The Hero** — the one who answers the call, faces the ordeal, brings something back. *Shadow:* the ego that can't admit defeat, the savior complex, war for glory.
- **The Lover** — eros, union, beauty, devotion. *Shadow:* addiction, possession, dissolving into the other and losing the self.
- **The Warrior** — boundary, courage, the disciplined will, the protective blade. *Shadow:* the brute; violence with no cause; the spear that won't be set down.
- **The Magician / Magus** — the transformer, the one who works between worlds (Mercury, the Magician card, the air working). *Shadow:* the manipulator, the power-tripper, magic as control over others.
- **The Sovereign (King / Queen)** — sovereignty, ownership, blessing, holding the center. *Shadow:* the tyrant, or the abdicated throne — power hoarded or power refused.

## The descent goddesses (the throughline of this whole place)

*One goddess, distributed — the one who rules the place you have to die to enter. This is the devotional floor under the descent that runs through every Foundation.*

- **Persephone** — abducted into the underworld, returns as its queen. The descent that *becomes* sovereignty.
- **Inanna → Ereshkigal** — the *ur*-katabasis: Inanna strips through seven gates, is killed, hung on a hook, returns. Ereshkigal is the dark sister who rules the destination. The template under every other descent.
- **The Mórrígan** — Irish sovereignty goddess of death, battle, and prophecy; the washer-at-the-ford, the crow over the dying. *(Use* Irish, *not "Celtic.")*
- **Kali** — death as the door to liberation; she dances on the corpse of the ego.
- **Lilith** — the exiled wild feminine who refused to lie beneath and left the garden rather than submit. The bright shadow, in person.
- **The Cailleach / Baba Yaga** — the death-crone, winter, the hag who eats and remakes. The one who devours what's done so the new can come.

## The four types (Toni Wolff — the persona patterns)

*Wolff's map of how the persona organizes, taught in CreativeMind. Each has a corresponding shadow. The point isn't to type yourself permanently — it's to find your dominant pattern and work its shadow first.*

- **Mother / Wife** *(masc: Father)* — *light:* nurturing, responsible, the rock. *Shadow:* smothering — or its opposite, selfish and unattached.
- **Lover** *(masc: Lover)* — *light:* passionate, creative, alive. *Shadow:* unmoored, commitment-averse.
- **Warrior / Amazon** *(masc: Warrior)* — *light:* achieving, capable, self-made. *Shadow:* cold, driven by fear of laziness, powerless underneath.
- **Mystic / Wise Woman** *(masc: Sage / Mystic)* — *light:* spiritual, intuitive, deep. *Shadow:* world-despising, bypassing, materially helpless.

## Find your own stack

The universal cast is the grammar. *Your* stack is the sentence it wrote — the specific handful of figures that recur in you, and the central tension between them. Here's mine, as a worked example (from my own work, not a prescription):

**The Architect** (must see the system first), **the Magician** (synthesizes across domains), **the Operator** (ships it, cuts through), **the Rebel** (guards sovereignty, refuses false authority), **the Teacher/Mentor**, and **the Seer** (knows before it can say how). And under all six, one tension running the whole show: **contain the fire, or express the fire.** The Architect and Operator want to contain; the Rebel and Magician want to let it rip; the Teacher and Seer know when each serves.

**Your turn:** over a month, watch for the figures that keep showing up — in your reactions, your dreams, the roles you fall into. Name three to six. Then find the single tension that runs *between* them. That tension is the engine of your individuation, and naming it is the first time you get to drive it instead of be driven.
