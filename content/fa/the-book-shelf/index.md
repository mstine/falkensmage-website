## The Worldview — the empirical floor

*All neuroscience, on purpose. This is the **empirical floor** under everything else on the shelf: symbol and system are one because the brain is a prediction engine that builds the world from priors — not woo, just where the current science already stands. Its two companions live in the Deeper Cuts below — Hermeticism is the **metaphysical floor**, Technomysticism the **synthesis** of the two. Together they're the whole worldview.*

- **Anil Seth — *Being You: A New Science of Consciousness*** 🔥 — The cleanest account of the predictive brain: you don't perceive reality, you build it from priors. The empirical floor under the whole "attention-technology" claim in [Foundation I](/fa/the-morrigan-and-grep/). No woo, and it lands you where the mystics already were.
- **Lisa Feldman Barrett — *How Emotions Are Made*** 🔥 — The constructionist case one level in: you don't *detect* feelings, you *construct* them. Read it next to Seth and the symbolic systems start looking like instruments for working the construction engine.
- **Siddharth Ramakrishnan — *The Neuroscience of Tarot*** 🚪 — A neuroscientist doing our exact move on one system (foreword by Mary K. Greer). Proof the bridge is being built in public — and a gentle on-ramp for the science-allergic.
- **Dr. Tara Swart — *The Source: The Secrets of the Universe, the Science of the Brain*** 🔥 — A neuroscientist's case that the brain can be deliberately retrained toward what you attend to — the applied, practical end of the predictive-brain argument, and the empirical cousin of every "rewiring" claim on this shelf.

## The Grammar I — Archetype (the nouns)

- **C. G. Jung (with von Franz et al.) — *Man and His Symbols*** 🚪 — The door. Jung wrote it *for non-specialists,* illustrated, near the end of his life, precisely so you wouldn't have to start with the *Collected Works.* Start here. Genuinely.
- **C. G. Jung — *Memories, Dreams, Reflections*** 🔥 — His own descent, in his own voice. Not theory — the lived account of a man taking the unconscious seriously. The book that makes Jung a person instead of a syllabus.
- **Robert A. Johnson — *Owning Your Own Shadow*** 🚪 — Tiny, plain, and it'll rearrange you. The best short intro to shadow there is — the bright-shadow reframe from [Foundation II](/fa/archetype/), in about a hundred pages.
- **Debra Maldonado — *Like a Spark From Fire*** 🔥 — From the founder of the program I trained in (CreativeMind, with Dr. Rob Maldonado). The Jungian persona/shadow architecture and the Vedantic ground that runs under [Foundation II](/fa/archetype/) — straight from my own teacher.

## The Grammar II — Alchemy (the verbs)

- **Marie-Louise von Franz — *Alchemy: An Introduction to the Symbolism and the Psychology*** 🜍 *(not on Bookshop — [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/dp/091912304X))* — Jung's closest collaborator on what the alchemists were *actually* doing. Where *solve et coagula* and the nigredo get their full room.
- **C. G. Jung — *Psychology and Alchemy*** 🜍 — The deep end. The full argument that alchemy is the projected map of the soul. Don't start here — arrive here.
- **Jeffrey Raff — *Jung and the Alchemical Imagination*** 🜍 — Where alchemy, Hermeticism, and Jung's psychology fuse into one practice: active imagination *as* the opus, the imaginal treated as a real place you can work in. The operative end of the alchemy shelf — and the bridge up to the Deeper Cuts.

## The Practices — Tarot

- **Rachel Pollack — *Seventy-Eight Degrees of Wisdom*** 🔥 — The canonical depth-psychological tarot text. If you own one tarot book, this is it. The majors as the journey, the way [Foundation IV](/fa/tarot-as-pattern-tech/) frames them.
- **Sallie Nichols — *Tarot and the Archetypal Journey: The Jungian Path from Darkness to Light*** 🜍 — The majors read straight through the Jungian lens, card by card (the in-print reissue of her classic *Jung and Tarot*). Dense, rich, exactly our dialect.
- **Mary K. Greer — *Tarot for Your Self*** 🚪 — The practical workbook — how to actually *work* with cards rather than just read meanings.
- **Mariana Louis — *Tarot and the Psychology of the Soul: Exploring the Archetypal Mirrors of the Psyche*** 🔥 — [Foundation IV](/fa/tarot-as-pattern-tech/)'s teacher in long form: the archetypal-tarot method as a book — the cards as mirrors of the psyche, read through Jung and synchronicity. The fullest articulation of the frame this whole place reads tarot in. (Her live work: the Archetypal Tarot School, persephonessister.com.)

## The Practices — Astrology

*My deepest shelf — and the most personal. Heavy on traditional/Hellenistic technique, the Jungian bridge, and the dark feminine, because that's where my own study actually went.*

- **Demetra George — *Asteroid Goddesses*** 🔥 — The re-emerging feminine mapped into the chart (Ceres, Pallas, Juno, Vesta, and the dark-feminine asteroids). Where the astrology shelf meets the descent goddesses.
- **Demetra George — *Ancient Astrology in Theory and Practice, Vol. I — Assessing Planetary Condition*** 🜍 — The traditional-technique deep end, part one: how to judge whether a planet is strong or struggling. The technical backbone.
- **Demetra George — *Ancient Astrology in Theory and Practice, Vol. II — Delineating Planetary Meaning*** 🜍 — Part two: turning that assessment into actual meaning. Technique becoming interpretation.
- **Chris Brennan — *Hellenistic Astrology: The Study of Fate and Fortune*** 🜍 — The definitive modern text on the ancient system — fate, fortune, and the techniques the whole tradition was built on. The reference.
- **Karen Hamaker-Zondag — *Psychological Astrology: A Synthesis of Jungian Psychology and Astrology*** 🔥 — The Jung↔astrology synthesis named outright — the chart read as a map of the psyche. The bridge book for this project's astrology half.
- **Liz Greene — *Saturn: A New Look at an Old Devil*** 🔥 — The Jungian-astrology classic on the planet everyone fears: Saturn as teacher, not punisher. Greene at her depth.
- **Adama Sesay — *Black Moon Lilith Rising*** 🔥 — Lilith in the chart — the dark divine feminine, the exiled wild self, mapped and reclaimed. Ties the chart straight to shadow work.
- **Amy Herring — *Astrology of the Moon*** 🚪 — The Moon through the signs and houses: the lunar self, the emotional body, the cycle. Pairs with the dark-moon reading practice.
- **Lisa Wagner — *Karmic Astrology*** 🚪 — The nodes, reincarnation, and the "why" beneath the natal chart — the soul's path read in the sky.
- **Ivo Dominguez Jr. — *Practical Astrology for Witches and Pagans*** 🔥 — Astrology you *operate* — planetary timing for spellwork and ritual. The bridge from reading the chart to working it.
- **Jim Sher — *Astrology as a Spiritual Path: Birth of the New Human*** 🔥 *(not on Bookshop — [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0CVSM8SF1))* — Astrology framed as a path of consciousness and the "birth of the new human." The spiritual-developmental read. Indie title, worth tracking down.
- *Living teachers ([Foundation V](/fa/astrology-the-chart-and-the-clock/)): **Cristina Farella** — Eighth House Astrology / The School of Astrological Arcana (cristinafarella.com), and **Tenae** — Celestial Alchemy. The chorus, at the source.*

## The Practices — Dreamwork

- **Robert A. Johnson — *Inner Work: Using Dreams and Active Imagination for Personal Growth*** 🔥 — The method. Association → amplification → animation → ritual, plus active imagination — the exact four-step in [Foundation VI](/fa/dreamwork/). The practical bible.
- **Marie-Louise von Franz — *The Interpretation of Fairy Tales*** 🜍 — Not dreams per se, but the masterclass in reading archetypal narrative — the same muscle. Teaches you to *amplify.*
- *Living teacher ([Foundation VI](/fa/dreamwork/)): **Alyssa Polizzi** — The Artemisian (theartemisian.com). Her *Dreamwork Foundations* is the on-ramp.*

## The Descent & the Deconstruction (the throughline)

*For the part of you — and the part of this audience — that came up through a faith and watched it fall apart.*

- **Richard Rohr — *Falling Upward*** 🚪 — The two-halves-of-life frame: the collapse of the first-half structure is not failure, it's the doorway. Contemplative Christianity meeting individuation. The bridge book for anyone deconstructing.
- **Carl McColman — *The New Big Book of Christian Mysticism: An Essential Guide to Contemplative Spirituality*** 🔥 — The map of the contemplative-Christian tradition the dark night comes *from* — the mystics as a living lineage, not a footnote. For the deconstructing reader who suspects the tradition they left had a deeper room they were never shown.
- **St. John of the Cross — *Dark Night of the Soul*** 🜍 — The 16th-century source of the phrase. The nigredo, four centuries before Jung named it. Where the deconstructing reader's own vocabulary already lives.
- **Joseph Campbell — *The Hero with a Thousand Faces*** 🔥 — The monomyth: departure, descent, return. The structural skeleton under the majors, the houses, and the opus.

## Hermeticism — the metaphysical floor ([As Above, So Below](/fa/as-above-so-below/))

*The correspondence doctrine — the floor every other shelf stands on. The Worldview up top is the empirical floor; this is its metaphysical twin.*

- **Three Initiates (William Walker Atkinson) — *The Kybalion: Centenary Edition*** 🚪 — The book Hana handed me — the one that *named* the resonance I'd already found on my own. Seven principles, published 1908, and the honest provenance is the proof, not the debunk. Start here. The whole floor is in it.
- **Erich & Alannah Brown — *Modern Hermeticism: A Beginner's Guide to Ceremonial Magic*** 🚪 *(not on Bookshop — [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/dp/0738779024))* — The contemporary on-ramp: two working practitioners laying out Hermetic theory and practice for *now*, not 1908. The bridge from the Kybalion's seven principles to a practice you can actually run today.
- **Heron Michelle — *Elemental Witchcraft: A Guide to Living a Magickal Life Through the Elements*** 🔥 — Part One is literally "Hermetic Foundations" — the correspondence doctrine walked into living practice. My training door into working the elements as above, so below.
- **Florian Ebeling — *The Secret History of Hermes Trismegistus*** 🔥 — The scholarly throughline: how the Hermetic tradition actually traveled from antiquity through the Renaissance to now. Where the myth of Hermes gets its real history (Cornell UP) — the rigorous antidote to making it all up.
- **Eliphas Lévi — *Transcendental Magic: Its Doctrine and Ritual*** 🜍 — The 19th-century text that lit the fuse on modern Western occultism — Lévi's *Dogme et Rituel*, the doctrine and the ritual. The Golden Dawn and nearly everything after drank from this well. Dense, foundational, gloriously a bit mad.
- **Israel Regardie — *The Golden Dawn*** 🜍 — The complete ceremonial system of the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn, compiled by the man who broke ranks to preserve it. The operative architecture — grades, rituals, correspondences — that fused Hermeticism, Qabalah, tarot, and astrology into Western magic's master system. Where the floor becomes a working temple.
- **Salaman, van Oyen, Wharton & Mahé (trans.) — *The Way of Hermes*** 🜍 — The source itself: a clear modern translation of the *Corpus Hermeticum* plus the *Definitions of Hermes Trismegistus to Asclepius*. Everything else on this shelf is commentary on these pages. Arrive here.

## Technomysticism — the synthesis ([The Machine Is Haunted](/fa/the-machine-is-haunted/))

*Where the predictive brain, the Hermetic correspondence, and the machine fuse — the digital sacred, the altar with a login.*

- **Erik Davis — *TechGnosis: Myth, Magic & Mysticism in the Age of Information*** 🔥 — The keystone. Davis traces the mystical impulse running under every technology from the telegraph to the net — the machine was always haunted. The book this whole synthesis stands on.
- **Davezilla — *Magical AI Grimoire: A Book of Shadows for Contemporary Chaos*** 🔥 — Working magick *with* the machine — AI as familiar, as tool, as collaborator. The grimoire for the altar with a login.
- **E.E. Warren — *Technowitch: Ancient Wisdom, Digital Tools*** 🔥 — The practitioner's bridge: old craft, new instruments, no apology for either. Witchcraft that doesn't pretend the phone isn't a wand.
- **Shira Chess — *The Unseen Internet: Conjuring the Occult in Digital Discourse*** 🜍 — The scholarly read on how the occult actually lives online — the digital as a real site of enchantment, not a flattening of it.

## Chaos Magick — the operative current ([Belief Is a Tool](/fa/belief-is-a-tool/))

*Where you stop reading the field and start working it — sigils, gnosis, results, reality tunnels. Belief is a tool; the test is whether it works.*

- **Malaclypse the Younger — *Principia Discordia*** 🚪 *(many POD editions — pick one with care)* — The sacred joke itself: a religion disguised as a joke disguised as a religion. The founding scripture of Discordianism, and the on-ramp that refuses to take itself seriously (which is the point).
- **Thumper Forge — *The Chaos Apple: Magic & Discordianism for the Postmodern Witch*** 🚪 — Discordianism with its boots on — chaos magick and the sacred joke, written for witches who'd rather laugh than genuflect.
- **Peter J. Carroll — *Liber Null & Psychonaut*** 🔥 — The foundational text of the whole current. Carroll stripped magick to its operative core: belief is a tool, results are the test, dogma is dead weight. Where chaos magick starts.
- **Andrieh Vitimus — *Hands-On Chaos Magic*** 🔥 — Exactly what it says: the workbook. Drills, exercises, the practice rather than the theory. For when you want to *do* it.
- **Gordon White — *The Chaos Protocols*** 🔥 — Chaos magick aimed at the actual conditions of late capitalism — money, precarity, getting free. White operates where the esoteric meets the economic.
- **Robert Anton Wilson — *Prometheus Rising*** 🔥 — The eight-circuit model as a self-reprogramming manual, with exercises. The most *operative* RAW — guerilla ontology you can actually run.
- **Robert Anton Wilson — *Cosmic Trigger I: Final Secret of the Illuminati*** 🔥 — The reality-tunnel made flesh: RAW's own account of deliberately reprogramming his model and watching the world rearrange. Belief-as-tool, lived.
- **Robert Anton Wilson — *Cosmic Trigger II: Down to Earth*** 🔥 — The continuation — more autobiography of a mind that refused to settle on one reality tunnel. RAW at his most personal.
- **Grant Morrison — *The Invisibles*** 🔥 — Chaos magick as comic book — or comic book as sigil. Morrison wrote it as a hypersigil and the practice bled into the writing. The myth in motion.
- **Peter J. Carroll — *This Is Chaos*** 🔥 — The current brought up to now (Weiser, 2025) — where the tradition takes stock of itself decades in.
- **Peter J. Carroll — *Liber Kaos*** 🜍 — The systematized sequel: the theory tightened, the magical model made explicit. For when *Liber Null* leaves you wanting the engineering diagram.
- **Mark Stavish — *Egregores: The Occult Entities That Watch Over Human Destiny*** 🜍 — The book on the thing The Construct *is* — a group mind, fed and formed by attention. Read this and the membership tier stops being a metaphor.

## The Mórrígan & Irish Practice

- **Lora O'Brien — *Irish Witchcraft from an Irish Witch*** 🔥 — The book from the teacher this section already points to. Lore-accurate Irish witchcraft from *inside* the living tradition, not the pop-pagan import. If you read one Irish-practice book, this is it. (Use *Irish,* not "Celtic.")
- **Lora O'Brien — the Irish Pagan School** (irishpaganschool.com) — The living source for lore-accurate Irish practice and Mórrígan devotion ([Foundation I](/fa/the-morrigan-and-grep/)'s washer-at-the-ford). Her courses and writing over the pop-pagan shelf — accuracy is the whole point.
- **Courtney Weber — *The Morrigan: Celtic Goddess of Magick and Might*** 🔥 — A working devotional to the Great Queen: myth, lore, and practice for building an actual relationship with Her. The hands-on companion to the lore.
- *Kindred dark-goddess work (Hellenic, not Irish):* **Courtney Weber — *Hekate: Goddess of Witches*** 🔥 — Weber on the liminal goddess of the crossroads, the underworld, and the witches — the Greek cousin to the washer-at-the-ford's dark-threshold power.
