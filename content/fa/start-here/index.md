Howdy, folks.

This is the orientation page for everything published here. As of mid-2026, there are roughly forty pieces in the field, and that's enough that a new reader could reasonably ask where to start.

The answer depends on what brought you here. Below are five tracks — five entrances into the same body of work — plus a short essentials list for the cold-open reader who wants the five must-reads regardless of track. Pieces appear in multiple tracks when they're doing multi-thematic work. That's deliberate; the body of work is cross-cutting.

Pick what grips you. Read in any order. Come back to the others when one of them surfaces in your life.
