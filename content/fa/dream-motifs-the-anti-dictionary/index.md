**Read this part before you read anything below it.**

This is *not* a dream dictionary. There is no lookup table where snake = X and teeth = Y, and if you use it as one you've done the exact thing [Foundation VI](/fa/dreamwork/) told you not to. A snake to a herpetologist is not a snake to a Pentecostal kid is not a snake to a witch who works with serpent medicine. **Your symbols are yours.** Nobody — not me, not an app, not this page — can hand you their meaning.

So here's what this actually is: a set of **launch points.** For each common motif, two things — the **archetypal territory** it *tends* to open (where the universal pattern usually lives), and a **starting question** that bounces you straight back into your *own* associations. Use the territory to orient. Use the question to begin. Then **leave this page** and go work the dream with the method from [Foundation VI](/fa/dreamwork/) — associate, amplify, animate. The motif is the trailhead. The walk is yours.

If you ever catch yourself reading these for "the answer," close the tab. You're doing it wrong, and gently, I mean that with love.

## The motifs

**Houses & buildings** — *Territory:* the psyche itself; the structure of your own interior. Different rooms, different parts of you. **Your question:** *Which room was I in, which one was I avoiding — and what part of me lives there?*

**Basements & attics** — *Territory:* below = the unconscious, the buried, the repressed (often the shadow); above = the intellect, the lofty, the stored-away. **Your question:** *What did I find down/up there, and how long has it been waiting?*

**A room I'd never noticed before** — *Territory:* unlived potential; a part of the self just becoming available. **Your question:** *What was in the room I didn't know my house had?*

**Water** — *Territory:* the unconscious and the emotional deep. Calm, flooding, frozen, drowning — the *state* is the message. **Your question:** *What was the water doing, and where in my life does it feel like that right now?*

**Being chased or pursued** — *Territory:* the disowned (often the *bright* shadow) trying to be seen; it chases because you keep running. **Your question:** *What's pursuing me — and what happens if I turn around and face it?* (Try the turn in active imagination. Watch what changes.)

**Teeth falling out** — *Territory:* commonly power, voice, potency, how you're "held together," or a fear of losing face — but this one is *especially* personal. **Your question:** *Where do I feel like I'm losing my grip, my bite, or my words?*

**Flying** — *Territory:* freedom, transcendence, rising above — or escape and avoidance of the ground. **Your question:** *Was I free up there, or fleeing something down here?*

**Falling** — *Territory:* loss of control, a descent (not always bad — sometimes the necessary *solve*), letting go. **Your question:** *What am I afraid to drop — or what's asking to come apart?*

**Nakedness in public** — *Territory:* exposure, authenticity, the persona slipping, being *seen.* **Your question:** *Where do I fear being seen as I actually am — and who was looking?*

**Death & dying** — *Territory:* almost never literal; the nigredo, an ending that precedes a becoming, an old self dissolving. **Your question:** *What in me is ending — and am I fighting it or letting it die clean?*

**The dead returning** — *Territory:* unfinished business, inheritance, a quality of theirs alive in you, the ancestral. **Your question:** *Who came back, and what did they come to say or hand me?*

**Snakes & serpents** — *Territory:* transformation (the shed skin), healing, kundalini, instinctual power — *or* threat, depending on you. **Your question:** *Does this snake feel like danger or like medicine — and what's that about for me?*

**Animals** — *Territory:* instinct, the part of you that isn't civilized — which animal, and how you relate to it, is the whole tell. **Your question:** *What does this specific creature do, fear, or want — and where does that live in me?*

**Doors, gates, thresholds, keys** — *Territory:* transition, choice, initiation, a between-state. **Your question:** *What am I on the threshold of — and did I go through, or stand there?*

**Vehicles & driving** (no brakes, backseat, lost the car) — *Territory:* your sense of agency and direction in life; who's actually steering. **Your question:** *Was I driving — and if not, who was, and where were we headed?*

**Being lost / can't find the way** — *Territory:* disorientation, a lost thread, the *via negativa,* not knowing — which is sometimes exactly where you're supposed to be. **Your question:** *What am I trying to find my way back to — and what if there isn't a map yet?*

**Lost or stolen wallet / ID / phone** — *Territory:* identity, how you're "verified," the persona-level question of who you are to others. **Your question:** *What part of how I prove who I am just went missing?*

**Babies & children** — *Territory:* the new self, a nascent project or potential, the inner child, the vulnerable just-born thing. **Your question:** *What's newly alive in me that needs protecting — or that I'm neglecting?*

**Fire** — *Territory:* transformation, destruction-and-renewal, passion, the alchemical heat of the opus, rage. **Your question:** *What's burning — and is it razing something or forging it?*

**The exam I didn't study for / showing up unprepared** — *Territory:* a feeling of being tested, judged, not-enough; the persona's fear of being found out. **Your question:** *Where do I feel unready to be evaluated — and whose judgment am I dreading?*

**Stairs, ladders, climbing or descending** — *Territory:* ascent (effort, aspiration, the heady) or descent (going down into the deep, the underworld, the body). **Your question:** *Was I climbing or descending — and toward or away from what?*
