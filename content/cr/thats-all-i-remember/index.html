It was the first and last time I took you to that church

You were tiny and peaceful and everyone loved you

And then service ... no clue what the sermon was about

It got so quiet... he said The Spirit is moving. Can you hear that?

You decide to answer. Crying. I squirm.

Can feel thousands of pairs of eyes. I try to comfort you.

MA'AM TAKE THE CHILD OUT THE SPIRIT IS TRYING TO MOVE

He really .... yelled that. I squirm. I try to comfort you.

MA'AM TAKE THE CHILD OUT NOW

He sends his MEN to escort us out

No clue what the sermon was about

That's. All. I. Remember...
