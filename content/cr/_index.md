title: "Contemplating Resonance"
lede: "Contemplating Resonance was a podcast and newsletter I ran from late 2023 to the spring of 2025. Three seasons of conversations with people who walked out of evangelicalism and kept going: into Anglican pews, pagan circles, chaos magick, Jungian depth work, and whatever else was waiting past the cage. Alongside it, an out-of-order memoir of faith, religious trauma, deconstruction, and the slow realization that my analytical mind and my mystical mind were never two different minds. ADHD burnout ended it in April 2025, and that's allowed. The thread never broke; it runs straight into Feral Architecture. The whole archive lives here now, on my own ground, every episode and every post."
