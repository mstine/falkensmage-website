I don’t feel safe.

Am I safe? For now.

Why am I safe? Because I work to keep myself safe.

I cannot rest.

But I need to rest.

I am so tired of working so hard to give myself the one thing no one should have to work to get.

Why can’t I be safe for free? It would be enough.

It would be plenty.
