…because if I don’t rip this bandaid off, I don’t know what it will take for me to start putting my words out there into the ether once again.

So, in the words of that famous Bill O’Reilly meltdown, FUCK IT, WE’LL DO IT LIVE.

Do what live? I’m literally writing an article that will be published asynchronously while I’m off doing something else. It’s the energy of the statement I’m channeling. The idea that, it doesn’t have to be perfect. We’ll just get it done on vibes. And that’s gonna be OK. I’m not sure if that’s what Bill was feeling in that moment (prolly not lol), but that’s what we’re going to go with.

We’re going to write what’s resonating this week on vibes.

And things were going pretty well until I finished that clever introduction. And then the channel just went blank. Much like O’Reilly’s teleprompter. Hah. It’s amazing what happens when you just roll with the synchronicity flow. Interesting connections that you never thought possible just come out of nowhere.

And that’s kinda what life has been like now for nearly a month. While yes, I must give a nod to the fact that the world is literally inside of a cocktail shaker getting the ride of its life, I’m doing really well.

My lovely Ego says I should feel guilty for that. Don’t tell people you are riding high while everything is burning down. What an asshole thing to say.

But it’s true.

And, as has been a recurring theme of the past several weeks, having fun and being filled with joy are both forms of RESISTANCE. The powers that be do not want us to be happy. They do not want us having fun. They want us scared, ungrounded, feeling vulnerable, energetically unregulated, and cowering in the corner.

It’s easier to control someone who is afraid.

It’s not easy to control someone who is grounded, standing in their power, and creating a beautiful reality for themselves.

So I’ll keep this short, sweet, and to the point. I did what I came here to do, which is to once again dip my quill into my inkwell and alchemize some words to share with y’all.

Your homework?

Do something that makes your heart sing today.

And then, just sit and bask in the glory of that music.

And when you’re ready, take a deep breath, smile, raise both of your middle fingers in the direction of those forces of oppression and speak this simple spell:

May they get what they deserve.

And so it is.

Blessed be.
